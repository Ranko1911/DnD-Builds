# Objetos a Obtener — Hurricane Monk

**Orden de prioridades:** `1` > `2` > `3` > `Alta` > `Media` > `Baja`

### ⚙️ Gestión de Ranuras de Sintonización (Max 3 Activas)

* **Tier 1 (Niveles 1-4):** *Bracers of Defense* (1 slot), *Eldritch Claw Tattoo* (1 slot). (Total: 2).
* **Tier 2 (Niveles 5-10):** *Bracers of Defense* (1 slot), *Ring of Protection* (1 slot), *Boots of Striding and Springing* (1 slot). (Total: 3).
* **Tier 3 (Niveles 11-16):** *Bracers of Defense* (1 slot), *Cloak of Displacement* (1 slot), *Ring of Free Action* (1 slot). (Total: 3).
* **Tier 4 (Niveles 17-20):** *Gloves of Soul Catching* (1 slot), *Cloak of Displacement* (1 slot), *Belt of Cloud Giant Strength* (1 slot). (Total: 3).
