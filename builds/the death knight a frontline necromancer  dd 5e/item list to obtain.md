# Objetos a Obtener — Death Knight: Frontline Necromancer | D&D 5e

**Orden de prioridades:** `1` > `2` > `3` > `Alta` > `Media` > `Baja`

### ⚙️ Gestión de Ranuras de Sintonización (Max 3 Activas)

* **Tier 1 (Niveles 1-4):** *Infused Half-Plate (+1)* (1 slot), *Infused Weapon (+1)* (1 slot). (Total: 2).
* **Tier 2 (Niveles 5-10):** *Headband of Intellect* / *Wand of the War Mage +2* (1 slot), *Cloak of Protection* (1 slot), *Ring of Protection* (1 slot). (Total: 3).
* **Tier 3 (Niveles 11-16):** *Staff of Defense* / *Staff of Power* (1 slot), *Cloak of Displacement* (1 slot), *Ring of Free Action* (1 slot). (Total: 3).
* **Tier 4 (Niveles 17-20):** *Staff of the Magi* (1 slot), *Armor of Invulnerability* (1 slot), *Tome of Clear Thought* (1 slot). (Total: 3).
